The screening blind spot

55.9% of men have never had their blood pressure measured — vs 32% of women. This fuels the unawareness crisis.

Men are 1.75x more likely to never get screened. Targeted outreach in barbershops, taxi ranks, and workplaces could save thousands. Mbeh 2019

What if we scaled awareness nationwide?

Currently, awareness among hypertensives is 14.1% → control rate only 3.5%. Each 1% absolute increase in awareness raises control by 0.4% (real-world meta-analysis). Baseline: ~9,200 preventable deaths/year (IHME 2021).

Awareness target (% of hypertensives): 14.1%
Model: control improvement slope 0.4 (derived from global hypertension management trials). Mortality reduction RR 0.65 from antihypertensive treatment meta-analysis (Lancet 2016). Baseline deaths: 9,200 (GBD 2021).

The remedy: A smart early-warning system

Community health workers log BP into a simple app. If elevated → SMS reminders & nurse alerts. No one falls through. This dashboard is the first prototype of a national surveillance tool.
