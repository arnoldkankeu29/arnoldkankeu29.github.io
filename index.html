My father was one of the 85.9%. His blood pressure was flagged twice, but no one followed up. He died at 47 from a preventable cardiac illness. This is why I want to work with health data – to build systems that catch warning signs before it's too late.
A simple solution: mobile-based tracking.
A community health worker logs BP readings into a basic app. If elevated, the system sends SMS reminders to the patient and alerts a nurse. No one falls through the cracks. This dashboard is my first step toward making that real.
